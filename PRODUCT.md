# Point

## Register

product

## Platform

ios

## Users

Anyone who wants walking navigation with a pointing glove. The team intends to market the app to people with visual impairments, so voice and nonvisual feedback are central to the first version.

## Product Purpose

Speak a destination, choose the matched place, then use phone GPS and a glove's orientation to find the next route beacon. A vibration confirms correct pointing. Hardware is being built by other teammates.

## Positioning

Say where you want to go, then feel where to point.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, conversational, and carefully animated. The user asked for one voice button that transitions into a beautiful map after a phrase such as “take me to Shake Shack.”

## Anti-references

Cluttered map dashboards. Use the existing route and geographic-beacon algorithm with a new minimal app interface; exclude camera, depth, streaming, and unrelated dashboard features.

## Design Principles

- One primary action per state.
- Show the destination before starting the journey.
- Vibration confirms pointing alignment, not a turn code or walking direction.
- Keep the map and voice interaction continuous.
- Keep hardware and vibration tuning replaceable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implement VoiceOver labels and state announcements, Dynamic Type, 44-point minimum controls, a typing alternative, and Reduce Motion. These are implementation choices supporting the audience, not a claim of completed accessibility certification. No camera-based obstacle detection is included.
